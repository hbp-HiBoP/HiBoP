# T10 - Structural and geometry coverage

## Objective
Implement identity/topology-sensitive D3–D9, D14, D16–D18 and D20 families.

## Shared context and dependencies
T09 handlers; operation matrix, wire and lifecycle contracts.

## Scope and preserved contracts
Typed checkpoints, stable and semantic automatic-cut IDs, resource barriers, original-topology sparse/bitset masks. Oversized mask body uses independent bulk while descriptor remains in scene-operation order.

## Out of scope

## Automated acceptance criteria
A1. Affected assemblies compile. A2. Both drivers prove identity survives non-last deletion/reorder and derived effects do not re-emit. A3. Resource/topology failures reject only the operation; barriers and large-mask routing are tested. D14 parity passes or its row remains blocked for product decision.

## Verification contract
Static gate, focused Fast/SceneFocused MCP tests; completed jobs, console, names/counts and timing required. D14 parity is a distinct manual/product gate.

## Human acceptance
Review D14 position parity or decide its blocked product row.

## Unresolved decisions
UNRESOLVED: D14 qualification/product decision, exact files/tests.

## Completion report
