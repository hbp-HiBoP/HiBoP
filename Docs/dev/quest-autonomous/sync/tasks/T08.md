### Implementation entry points

- `DesktopSceneCapture` creates the Desktop v2 publication owner at the capture boundary.
- `QuestManager` retries an unsupported in-transfer mutation from a fresh capture with a new transfer identity.
- `QuestAnatomySession` preserves legacy wrapper handling while routing v2 records to the Quest owner; v2 does not create the experimental `DesktopReplicaSession` owner.
- `DesktopV2ReplicaSession` and `QuestV2ReplicaSession` own the initial journal/checkpoint cutover and early Quest apply.
- `DesktopV2ReplicaSession` starts its reconnect supervisor while the initial barrier is pending. It reuses the publication owner and persistent transport during the 500 ms grace, and only reports the scene live after receiving the matching initial barrier acknowledgement.
- `QuestV2ReplicaSession` retains completed checkpoint bytes, the bounded deferred-record queue, and its byte count on the same Quest owner. It resumes checkpoint application and drains records in arrival order before reading new records after reconnect; each record is removed immediately after processing succeeds, so a later disconnect cannot repeat an already-applied mutation.
- `MRIManager.Select` and `SelectPrepared` notify the Desktop publication owner. The owner cancels the current initial delivery; the retry captures the full scene again.
- Desktop sends schema-3 proposal rejection or correction records to Quest. The Quest owner applies targeted corrections; a rejection without a usable correction ends online optimistic mutation mode rather than retaining a divergent value as online.
- The active Quest v2 receive path records transport first/last-byte times, apply start/end, and next-frame visibility eligibility for color, complete cut definitions, and timeline anchors. Logical/capture identity components absent from the wire remain unavailable.
