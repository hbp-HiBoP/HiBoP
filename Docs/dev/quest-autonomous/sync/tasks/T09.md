## Accepted timeline clock policy

The clock estimator is used only while at least one timeline is in `play`:

- Probe every 250 ms while playback is active. When all timelines are paused, retain the transport's low-rate liveness ping but do not admit its samples to the clock estimator.
- Retain up to 32 samples in a rolling two-second window; require at least three samples and a newest sample no older than one second.
- Among samples no older than one second, select the lowest-RTT sample; reject estimates with uncertainty above half a sample.
- While playing, correct only when drift exceeds one sample plus the estimate uncertainty.
- The anchor carries an explicit intent through publication, the wire payload and application. Only `Play` uses the estimator. If its estimate is unusable, apply the transmitted index exactly and start playback on receipt, including at the final index of a non-looping timeline. `Pause`, `Seek`, `Step` and `Loop` always apply the transmitted index exactly and never call the estimator, even when the timeline remains in play after a seek, step or loop edit.

Time uses monotonic 64-bit ticks with declared frequency. Wall-clock time is not synchronization or conflict authority.
