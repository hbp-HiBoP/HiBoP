## Completion report
- **Desktop to Quest:** `V2SceneMutationBoundary.MutationProposed` feeds `V2DesktopMutationAuthority`, which assigns acceptance-order canonical sequences and publishes `CanonicalReady`; `V2QuestMutationDriver.ReceiveCanonical` applies the typed color, cut, or timeline setter once. `DesktopCanonicalMutations_ConvergeForColorCutAndTimeline` covers three canonical edits.
- **Quest to Desktop:** `V2QuestMutationDriver.ApplyOptimistic` applies through the prepared boundary and queues a reliable Quest proposal with its observed canonical sequence. `V2DesktopMutationAuthority.AcceptQuestProposal` admits it through the ledger, applies it on Desktop, then echoes the canonical value. `QuestOptimisticProposals_ConvergeAndMatchingEchoesDoNotApplyTwice` covers all three mutation families and confirms matching echoes do not call the setters twice.
- **Lost ACK and idempotency:** `LostCanonicalAcknowledgement_RetryReusesFrameAndDoesNotDoubleApply` verifies identical reliable-frame replay and cached duplicate canonical output.
- **Stale and correction ordering:** `StaleSameKeyProposalGetsDesktopCorrectionWhileDisjointProposalIsAccepted` verifies a current same-key correction while a stale proposal on another key is admitted. `MatchingCorrectionWatermark_PreventsOlderCanonicalFromRollingBackOptimisticValue` covers an equal optimistic/correction value followed by an older same-key canonical.
- **Reconnect grace:** `GraceReconnectIsSilentAndExpiryAbandonsHistoryWithoutChangingTheView` reconnects at 499 ms with the same frame and no offline notice, then expires at 500 ms, abandons the pending operation, and retains the optimistic scene value.
